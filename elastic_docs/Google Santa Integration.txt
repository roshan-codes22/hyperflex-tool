The Google Santa integration collects and parses logs from
Google Santa
, a security tool for macOS that monitors process executions and can blacklist/whitelist
binaries.
Compatibility
The Google Santa integration was tested with logs from Santa 2022.4.
Google Santa is available for MacOS only.
The integration is by default configured to read logs from
/var/db/santa/santa.log
.
